Approach

Three questions I ask before any trade.

Every project below is built around a classic way strategies fail. Each one is a working Python model, pulls data from Yahoo Finance and runs in your browser. Change the inputs and the analysis re-computes.

01 / CORRELATION

What do I lose in a bad month?

Diversification works until correlations rise toward one. Then VaR grows faster than intuition expects, because the hedge disappears just when you need it.

02 / STATIONARITY

Is the relationship real, and does it last?

Two charts that look alike are not a trade. I need a cointegration test, a half-life I can afford and a stop-loss for the day the relationship breaks.

03 / HONEST TESTING

Would this have made money, for real?

Execution delays, fees and slippage reduce returns. A strategy has to beat buy & hold after costs, without look-ahead bias, or it has no reason to exist.
