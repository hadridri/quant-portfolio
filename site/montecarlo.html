Key terms

Monte Carlo

Simulate thousands of random futures with the same volatility and correlations as the real stocks, then look at the results.

VaR 95%

The loss you should not exceed in 95% of months. One month in twenty is worse.

Expected shortfall

The average loss in that worst 5%. It shows how bad a bad month really gets.

Correlation

When stocks fall together, diversification stops working and the risk rises.
