Key terms

Cointegration

Two prices that drift apart but always come back together. Tested with Engle-Granger: p-value below 0.05 = linked.

Z-score

The gap between the two stocks, in standard deviations from its recent average.

Half-life

How many days the gap takes to close by half. Between 5 and 30 days is tradeable.

Hedge ratio

The proportion of A and B that makes the position neutral to the market.
