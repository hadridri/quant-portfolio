Key terms

SMA

Simple moving average: the average closing price over the last 50 or 200 days.

Golden cross

SMA 50 crosses above SMA 200: the trend turns up. Death cross is the opposite.

Drawdown

The loss from the highest point reached. Max drawdown is the worst one.

E-mail alert

Every weekday, a script checks SPY, QQQ, AAPL, MSFT, NVDA and GLD and sends an e-mail on each new cross.
